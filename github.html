About me

I am a civil engineer with hands-on site experience in progress tracking, reporting and coordination. My major is Structural Engineering, and I graduated from Cebu Technological University in 2024 as a DOST scholar.

I also hold a Master Plumber license and a Safety Officer II certification, so I can handle the structure, the plumbing and the safety side of a job.

I prefer to keep proven site practices and add technology only where it saves time or prevents errors.

That is how I work: keep what already works on site, then use digital tools to remove rework and repetitive reporting.
